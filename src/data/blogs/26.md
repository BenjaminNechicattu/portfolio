Just past midnight.

Outside, it was raining. I was watching Malayalam Movie Dose, and a romantic song was playing in the background. One of those nights where everything feels a little more emotional than usual.

Then my phone lit up.

A notification.

>    "Gdnit"
>
>    "Lv u..."
>
>    "Bye"

For a second...
I froze.
Was this really happening?
I stared at the screen, almost afraid to blink.

The notifications changed.

>    "Tata"
>    
>    "Bye"

The first messages had disappeared.
Deleted.
I had only seen them through the notification.
My heart was racing.
Was I dreaming?
Should I reply?

What if those messages weren't meant for me?
What if she had accidentally sent them... and realized her mistake?

Or...

What if they were meant for me, but she regretted sending them?
I didn't know which answer scared me more.

I was literally shivering.
Not the poetic kind.
The real kind.
Because it was her.

'The girl who likes Yellow.'

For a few seconds, every effort I had ever made suddenly felt worthwhile.
Maybe the roses weren't forgotten.
Maybe the handmade gifts meant something.
Maybe...
There was hope.

Then reality interrupted.
Why did she delete them?
Should I ask her?
No...

She had already made it clear she didn't want to talk about us anymore.
So I stayed silent.

And that silence became louder than the notification itself.
I wonder.

Were those words ever meant for me?
Or did I accidentally witness a moment that belonged to someone else?
Maybe I'll never know.
And perhaps that's why this memory still lives rent-free in my mind.

Hope can be beautiful.


After those notifications disappeared, my mind refused to stay quiet.
It started writing conversations that never happened.
Questions she never asked.

Answers I never got to give.

If she had asked me,
"Do you love me? Why?"
I already knew my answer.

Of course, I do.

Because you make me feel like nobody else does.

You make me feel comfortable.

You make me smile without trying.

You make me glow.

You make me more confident.

You make me feel seen.

You make me feel free.

You make me feel safe.

You make me feel like a winner, even on ordinary days.

You give me courage when I don't have any.

You make me believe I can take on the whole world.

You make my worries feel lighter.

You make me dream about a future I never planned before.

You make me proud of the person I become around you.

You are the reason I found the courage to be brave.

You are the reason I discovered what it feels like to fall in love.

Those words never left my lips.
They lived only inside my head.

And after those WhatsApp notifications...

She suddenly felt closer.

Not because anything had changed between us.
But because, for a few beautiful seconds, my heart believed the distance between us was finally getting smaller.
Sometimes, hope doesn't need proof.
Sometimes, it only needs three deleted messages.
But when it is built on uncertainty...

It becomes a beautiful disaster.
