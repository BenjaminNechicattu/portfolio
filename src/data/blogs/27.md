# In Pursuit of Love

## Chapter 11: *The Answer*

A few weeks passed.

But one question refused to leave my mind.
I had to know the truth behind the **Beautiful Disaster**.
Those three deleted messages.

Who were they for?
Why were they deleted?
Were they really... for me?

Every day I thought about asking.
Every day I convinced myself not to.

How do you ask someone if they accidentally confessed their love to you?

What if I was right?
What if I was wrong?
Neither answer felt easy.
That day finally came.

If, by some miracle, those messages had really been meant for me... I had no idea what I would do next.
But I carried something with me.
A small handmade promise.
The one I had written months ago.

> *On this Promise Day,*
>
> *I want you to know that,*
>
> *No matter where you are,*
>
> *No matter what time it is,*
>
> *You can come to me,*
>
> *And I promise to stand by your side.*
>
> *You will never have to face*
>
> *anything, anytime, anywhere,*
>
> *alone.*

Beautiful, isn't it?

At least, I thought so.
It took me hours of wandering through shops just to gather enough courage to ask one simple question.

Funny enough...

It was raining again.
Another coincidence.

No movie this time.
No romantic song.
Just me...

...and a question that had been living in my head for weeks.

So I asked.
"What did those messages mean?"

She looked at me for a second and smiled.

"Oh..."

"They were for my cousin."

"It was a mistake."

"I thought you didn't see them."

"Why didn't you ask me then?"

Silence.
Not outside.
Inside.

Everything I had imagined over those few weeks collapsed in a single sentence.

The theories.

The hope.

The late-night overthinking.

Gone.

Looking back now...

It's almost funny.
Who would write an entire love story from three deleted notifications?
Apparently...

Me. 😂

But maybe that's what love does.
It doesn't always make us irrational.
It makes us hopeful.
And hope has an incredible talent for filling in the blanks.
Sometimes, the truth isn't heartbreaking because someone rejected you.
Sometimes, it's heartbreaking because you have to say goodbye to a story that only ever existed in your own head.

The Beautiful Disaster finally had an answer.

It just wasn't the one I had been hoping for.

Just like Gods, Neither  Love Exists, its all an illution.
